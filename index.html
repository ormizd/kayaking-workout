Post-Workout Recovery

Fuel &Recover

High-protein meals designed to replenish glycogen, reduce inflammation, and rebuild muscle after time on the water.

⏱ Eat within 30–60 min after your workout
🫀
Meal 01
Barbecued Lamb Heart
~25g Protein Iron · B12 · CoQ10 Complex Carbs
Ingredients
✓
100 g lamb heart — marinated in garlic, lemon, olive oil & rosemary
✓
150 g roasted sweet potato — replenishes glycogen
✓
Large handful grilled zucchini & capsicum — antioxidants, fibre
✓
Small green salad with lemon dressing
✓
Glass of water or electrolyte drink
🐟
Meal 02
Canned Tuna in Olive Oil
~27g Protein Omega-3s Anti-Inflammatory
Ingredients
✓
100 g tuna in olive oil, drained
✓
150 g cooked brown rice or quinoa — sustained energy, complete amino acids (if quinoa)
✓
Half an avocado — healthy fats & potassium
✓
Sliced cucumber, cherry tomatoes & red onion
✓
Squeeze of lemon & cracked black pepper
🍗
Meal 03
Barbecued Chicken Breast
~32g Protein Lean Protein Low Fat · Digestible
Ingredients
✓
100 g chicken breast — marinated in yogurt, garlic & paprika
✓
150 g steamed or boiled baby potatoes
✓
Steamed broccoli & green beans — vitamin C, fibre, antioxidants
✓
Tablespoon of hummus or tzatziki on the side
⚡ Recovery Tips — All Meals
Eat within 30–60 minutes after your workout for best recovery and muscle protein synthesis.
Always include a carb source alongside protein to replenish muscle glycogen stores.
Stay well hydrated — aim for at least 500 ml of water around your workout.
Feel free to add an olive oil drizzle to any of the meals for extra healthy fats.
